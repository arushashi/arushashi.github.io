About

I am a Principal Site Reliability Engineer who started in desktop and server support in 2008 and grew through Windows infrastructure, Microsoft enterprise support and application production support into SRE. Today I lead reliability for customer-facing, device-telemetry, data-platform and internal services, run P1/P2 major-incident bridges, and introduced SLI/SLO and error-budget practice that several platform teams adopted.

I care about observability over plain monitoring, automation over manual recovery, and blameless postmortems that lead to real fixes. Recently I have been monitoring agentic AI applications (RAG, pgvector, LLMs) and building AI agents for operations.
